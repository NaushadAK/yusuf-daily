Top story

Paraguay's ousted president is placed under house arrest after a bank collapse

Santiago Peña was impeached on Friday and, shortly afterwards, confined to his home while prosecutors investigate him on suspicion of breach of trust. The case grows out of the collapse of the digital bank ueno, which left millions of customers locked out of their accounts. Vice President Pedro Alliana has been sworn in as president and will serve until the term ends in August 2028. Protesters gathered outside Peña's residence in Asunción.

India 04

  • Supreme Court refuses to soften its OBC creamy layer ruling

    The Centre had asked that the court's 11 March judgment apply only from now on. The court declined, repeating that a parent's income alone cannot decide creamy layer status, while protecting existing appointments. The government remains free to frame its own policy within the law laid down.

Something to think about

A possible new dwarf planet beyond Pluto

Images from the Vera Rubin Observatory, reported on 2 November, may show a previously unknown dwarf planet far beyond Pluto. It is an early result that astronomers will need to confirm with more observations.

The Solar System still has unseen worlds, and a new telescope that photographs the whole sky again and again is exactly the tool to find them.

Question for the day: how would you prove that a faint dot is a real world circling the Sun and not a star or a camera glitch?
