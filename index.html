Top story

Jensen Huang returns as Nvidia chief, and its shares jump 31% in a day

Nvidia's board voted unanimously on Monday to bring back Jensen Huang as chief executive, just 11 days after he resigned. The board said it had received input from senior levels of the US government, and reports say President Trump helped persuade it. Nvidia's shares closed about 31% higher, adding roughly $950 billion to its value, which reports call the biggest one-day gain in market value for any company. The news lifted the whole US market to its best day since April 2025, though analysts note that the company's internal investigation is still going on.

Something to think about

Is there a hidden planet at the edge of our Solar System?

Far beyond Neptune, small icy bodies follow strange, tilted orbits. Some astronomers have run computer simulations covering billions of years and found that an unseen planet, perhaps one and a half to three times Earth's mass and 250 to 500 times farther from the Sun than we are, could explain those patterns. Nobody has seen it yet; the clue is only its gravity.

This is how Neptune itself was found in 1846: astronomers noticed Uranus drifting off its predicted path, calculated where an unknown planet must be, and found it within a degree of the spot. Engineers use the same idea every day, working out a hidden force or fault from the way a machine or structure behaves.

Question for the day: If you could only measure how other objects move, how would you decide whether a hidden planet is real or whether our maths is simply missing something?
