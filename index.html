Cabinet clears new women's reservation bill with 50% more Lok Sabha seats

The Union Cabinet on Friday approved a fresh constitutional amendment bill that links women's reservation to delimitation, the redrawing of constituencies. Reports say every state would get the same 50% rise in its number of Lok Sabha seats, so that no state's share of the House shrinks, with each state's share then frozen for years to come. The aim is to bring in one-third reservation for women in the Lok Sabha and state assemblies before the 2029 general election. The bill is expected to come up in a short special sitting of Parliament that begins on Monday, 30 November. For Kerala and other southern states, which had worried about losing influence under a population-based redraw, the equal-increase formula is the key point to watch.

Something to think about

A sound-wave patch that helps you dream sooner

Engineers at the University of Texas at Austin have built a soft patch, called NEUSLeeP, that sits on the head and sends gentle ultrasound waves into deep parts of the brain while built-in electrodes read brain activity. In a small study of 28 people, published in Nature Communications, wearers reached REM sleep, the dreaming stage, about 43 minutes sooner and spent roughly 16 extra minutes in it compared with a fake treatment.

It works without drugs or surgery, and volunteers found it comfortable. But the trial was small, so bigger studies are needed before anyone can say it is safe and useful to wear every night.

Question for the day: Ultrasound has to pass through skin and bone to reach the brain. What would you need to think about when choosing the frequency and power of the waves so that they reach deep enough without heating anything?
