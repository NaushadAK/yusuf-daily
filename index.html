Top story

Xi greets Trump on the tarmac in Shenzhen ahead of three-way talks with Putin

US President Donald Trump arrived in Shenzhen yesterday for the APEC summit, and Chinese President Xi Jinping met him personally at the foot of Air Force One. It was an unusual gesture, returning the airport welcome Trump gave Xi in Washington in September. Russian President Vladimir Putin is also in Shenzhen, and the three leaders are expected to hold talks on the sidelines of the summit today, a meeting with no recent precedent. A peace deal for Ukraine and nuclear arms are expected to be on the table, though reports from the US suggest Putin is not ready for serious peace negotiations.

Something to think about

Voyager 1 is now one light-day from home

Yesterday NASA's Voyager 1, launched in 1977, became the first human-made object to reach one light-day from Earth: about 25.9 billion km. A radio command sent from Earth now takes a full 24 hours to arrive, and the reply takes another 24 hours to come back. It took the spacecraft about 49 years to get there.

Voyager runs on a nuclear power source that weakens every year, so engineers have switched off heaters and instruments one by one to keep it alive. Its computers have less memory than a modern car key fob, yet the team still sends software fixes across the gap.

Question for the day: If you had to fix a machine you could never touch, and every message took a day to reach it, how would you test your fix before sending it?
