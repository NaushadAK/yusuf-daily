Spain mourns 68 dead after its second high-speed rail disaster this year

A Renfe train carrying nearly 400 passengers ran into the back of a stationary Iryo train near Urda, in Toledo province, on Wednesday. Dozens more were injured, rescuers were still searching the carriages for survivors on Thursday, and the country has begun three days of mourning. The crash has reopened questions about the safety of Spain's rail network.

Something to think about

A common salamander has been glowing all along

Researchers found that Europe's black-and-yellow fire salamanders glow green and cyan under ultraviolet light. People have studied this animal for centuries, yet nobody noticed, because nobody looked with the right light.

In the same week, another team reported that spiders appear to sleep in stages, including something like the REM sleep linked to dreaming in humans.

Question for the day: what in your own field is in plain sight but needs a different "light" (a new dataset, a new tool, a new angle) to be seen?
