Trump, Xi and Putin to meet together in China on Thursday

The White House and the Kremlin say US President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin will hold their first-ever three-way meeting on 19 November, on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen. US officials say the main topic will be cutting nuclear weapons, and that the war in Ukraine is not on the agenda. China has not yet publicly confirmed the meeting, and Ukraine's President Zelenskiy has warned against big powers dividing the world into "spheres of influence".

Something to think about

A catalyst that protects itself while it works

Cobalt phosphide is a cheap material used to split water and make hydrogen fuel. Computer models said it should corrode and dissolve during the reaction, yet in real experiments it lasts well. A new study in the Journal of the American Chemical Society explains why: while it works, its surface gets covered by a thin layer of molecules from the reaction itself, and that layer shields the metal underneath from attack.

The researchers also combed through about 2,282 measurements from around 700 earlier papers to check the pattern. It is a good reminder that a material's surface can behave very differently once a process is running than it does on a lab bench.

Question for the day: Engineers protect steel from rust with paint, galvanising and oil films. Can you think of a metal that, like this catalyst, forms its own protective skin, and why that skin stops further corrosion?
