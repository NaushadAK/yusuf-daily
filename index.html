Israel's president hands Gadi Eisenkot the first chance to form a government

On Sunday, President Isaac Herzog gave Eisenkot, who leads the opposition Yashar party, the mandate to try to build a coalition. Eisenkot was recommended by 67 of the 120 Knesset members, against 46 for Prime Minister Netanyahu. He has 28 days, which can be extended by 14. Herzog cautioned that the numbers alone do not settle who can govern, because parties on both sides refuse to work with each other. Eisenkot says he wants a "national emergency government" and may try a minority government first.

Something to think about

Why do octopuses have three hearts?

Two of an octopus's hearts pump blood through the gills, and the third sends it around the rest of the body. Octopus blood uses copper rather than iron to carry oxygen, which makes it blue and works well in cold water, though it carries oxygen less efficiently than our blood.

Because of this, swimming makes the main heart pause, and the octopus tires quickly and prefers to crawl.

Question for the day: what trade-offs does a machine face when it has to push fluid through a long set of pipes, and could two small pumps beat one big one?
