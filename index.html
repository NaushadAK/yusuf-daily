Houthis claim attack on Saudi Arabia's biggest refinery at Ras Tanura

Yemen's Houthi group said on Wednesday that it attacked Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery and caused fires there. The Saudi-led coalition said its air defences destroyed four drones over Ras Tanura but did not confirm a hit on the plant, though satellite images reviewed by Reuters showed thick black smoke over a wide area around it. Ras Tanura can refine about 550,000 barrels of oil a day and sits next to a major crude export terminal. The claim follows a Houthi strike on Riyadh's financial district, and the coalition has hit back at the Houthi-held port of Hodeidah. Attacks on Gulf oil sites matter to India, which buys much of its crude from the region and has millions of citizens working there.

Something to think about

The mechanical engineer from Kozhikode who made India the world's biggest milk producer

Today is also National Milk Day, marking the birthday of Verghese Kurien, born in Kozhikode on 26 November 1921. Kurien trained as a mechanical engineer, first in Chennai and later in the United States, and was sent to a government creamery in Anand, Gujarat, almost by accident. There he helped farmers build the Amul cooperative and later led Operation Flood, which linked village milk collection centres to cities across India.

Much of that success was an engineering problem: milk spoils within hours, so it had to be collected twice a day, chilled quickly, carried in insulated tankers and processed in plants. Kurien's team also backed the world's first process for making milk powder from buffalo milk. Today India produces more milk than any other country.
