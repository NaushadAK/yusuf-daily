Top story

Putin agrees to keep three-way Ukraine talks going

After more than three hours with US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner at the Kremlin, President Putin agreed to continue the three-way talks between Russia, Ukraine and the US, according to his aide Yuri Ushakov, who said a new round in Abu Dhabi was possible. Putin also repeated that sending Western troops into Ukraine would be unacceptable to Russia. No breakthrough was announced, and the envoys are now taking the discussions to the Ukrainian side.

Something to think about

A material that flashes blue when you scratch it

Chemists at the University of Missouri have made a solid material that responds to three different things. It changes colour under light, reacts chemically when heated, and gives off a flash of blue light when its surface is scratched. They did it by carefully arranging how the molecules pack together, which decides how the material reacts to light, heat and force.

Materials that glow when stressed are called mechanoluminescent, and engineers are interested in them as built-in damage sensors. Imagine a coating on a bridge, an aircraft wing or a pressure vessel that lights up where a crack starts, long before you could see the crack.

Question for the day: If you painted a machine part with a coating that glows under stress, where on a simple shaft or bracket would you expect it to glow first, and why?
