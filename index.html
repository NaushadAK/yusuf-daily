The Yusuf Daily

Top story

Wall Street plunges as an AI and chip sell-off erases the post-election rally

US stocks fell sharply on Thursday. The Nasdaq dropped 5.52% into correction territory, the S&P 500 lost 4.36% for its worst day since April 2025, and the Dow shed more than 1,800 points. The Dow had surged about 1,500 points a day earlier after the midterms, and investors are now questioning how far the spending on AI will pay off. Indian markets had closed strongly before the US fall, so Friday's opening will be watched closely.

World in brief 05

  • Paraguay's president resigns, then is impeached

    Santiago Peña stepped down after a digital bank linked to his government collapsed, leaving about 3 million customers without access to their accounts. Lawmakers voted to impeach him within minutes, and prosecutors have opened criminal cases. Reports differ on whether the resignation has been formally accepted.

  • Ukraine aid centre strike: toll reaches 42

    Police say the death toll from the Russian strike has risen to 42. Turkey's President Erdogan, after meeting Zelenskiy, said he wants to discuss ending the war with Putin.

  • Iran braces as a tanker is hit off Qatar

    Iran is preparing for the possibility of fresh US and Israeli attacks now that the midterms are over. Separately, the British military says a tanker was struck by projectiles off Qatar's coast in the Gulf.

  • WHO approves trials of two new Ebola vaccines

    The vaccines are designed for the latest deadly strain of the virus. Trials are the next step before any wider use.

  • Colombia toughens terms for armed groups that surrender

    Congress passed a bill from President Abelardo de la Espriella that replaces the earlier "total peace" approach with stricter conditions.

India 05

  • Supreme Court stays contempt case against Punjab's Chief Secretary

    The proceedings concerned unpaid dearness allowance to state employees and pensioners. Punjab says it inherited about ₹13,000 crore in arrears, and the bench asked for a plan to pay pending amounts by March 2027.

  • The 24-hour rule starts from detention, not formal arrest

    The Supreme Court held that the period for producing a person before a magistrate runs from the time of detention.

  • Court questions an earlier ruling on bail under the NDPS Act

    A two-judge bench voiced reservations about a judgment saying the right to a speedy trial cannot override the Act's strict bail conditions.

  • Election Commission explains how deleted voters can return

    Voters removed during the special roll revision can use Form 6, and the Commission says no penalty applies. This followed a Supreme Court suggestion not to force about 4.3 million deleted Delhi voters to reapply as new voters.

  • Tirupati ghee trial stays in Andhra Pradesh

    The Supreme Court declined to move the temple ghee adulteration trial out of the state.

Kerala 03

  • Kochi airport bucks a fall in cargo exports

    A study of July to September found exports down about 15% at Thiruvananthapuram and lower at Calicut and Kannur, while Kochi rose 21%. Overall state cargo exports grew 3.8%.

  • Arrests in Kochi doctor robbery case

    Police arrested actress Athulya Chandra and actor Bibin Perumpally. A 41-year-old doctor was allegedly lured to a flat on 2 November and robbed of valuables worth ₹3.25 lakh; police are still looking into the motive.

  • High Court reinstates Milma's Thiruvananthapuram board

    A Division Bench reportedly struck down the government order dissolving the elected board, which followed a dispute over ghee supplied for Sabarimala rituals. This comes from a single report, so details may be updated.

  • Hockey: India beat Japan 4–1 to win the Asian Champions Trophy

    Japan scored first in the third minute, but India hit back in Mohali with goals from Shilanand, Dilpreet, Jugraj and Vivek. It is India's record sixth title.

  • Cricket: second ODI in Wellington tomorrow, rain a worry

    India lead the series 1–0. The match is on Saturday 7 November and is reported to start at 7:30 am IST. Forecasts suggest a high chance of rain.

  • Europa League: Juventus, Benfica and Marseille win

    Juventus won 2–1 at AZ Alkmaar, Benfica beat Lech Poznań 3–1 and Marseille won 1–0 at Leverkusen. Bournemouth won 3–1 at Sparta Prague.

  • Tennis: Paris Masters quarter-finals begin today

    Ben Shelton beat Arthur Rinderknech in three sets and plays Tommy Paul. Play starts at midday local time, about 4:30 pm IST.

Something to think about

Cold War ice samples reveal a much warmer Greenland

Scientists studying forgotten samples drilled in Greenland during the Cold War report that summers there about 416,000 years ago were roughly 8.5°F (almost 5°C) warmer than today.

Old material can answer new questions: samples kept in storage for decades are now being read with modern instruments.

Question for the day: if you were designing a sensor to record temperature for the next 400,000 years, what would you build it from, and how would a future scientist trust its readings?
