Cyclone Akvan takes shape over the Bay of Bengal

The deep depression in the Bay of Bengal has become Cyclone Akvan. In its 2:45 am bulletin today, the IMD placed the storm almost stationary about 610 km east of Chennai and 670 km south-southeast of Visakhapatnam. It is expected to grow into a very severe cyclonic storm and cross the Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Tuni around Friday, with heavy to extremely heavy rain likely in coastal Andhra on Thursday and Friday. District officials in Andhra have been put on high alert, and rain is also expected in parts of Tamil Nadu, Odisha and Bengal.

Something to think about

Light that behaves like a solid and a liquid at once

Physicists have turned light into a "supersolid", a strange quantum state that holds a regular, crystal-like pattern like a solid but can also flow without any friction like a liquid. It sounds impossible, because in everyday life a thing is either rigid or runny, never both.

Engineers already use the difference between solids and fluids all the time, from bearings and lubricants to cooling systems. Exotic states like this help scientists understand matter at its most basic level, and may one day feed into new kinds of computing.

Question for the day: Toothpaste, sand and custard can all act a bit like solids and a bit like liquids. Can you think of a machine part where you would want something to be stiff one moment and flow the next?
