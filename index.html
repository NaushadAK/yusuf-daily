Top story

Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first three-way talks in Shenzhen

The leaders of the US, China and Russia met together for the first time yesterday, on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen. The closed-door session lasted about 55 minutes, and Putin said at the start that Ukraine would be the main subject; nuclear arms and the conflict involving Iran were also expected to come up. Trump held separate meetings with Xi and Putin beforehand. Officials had warned not to expect major agreements, and so far the fact that the meeting happened at all is the main news.

India 05

  • Cyclone Akvan to cross the Andhra coast around noon today

    The IMD expects Akvan to reach land between Nellore and Tuni as a very severe cyclonic storm, with winds of 120–130 km/h gusting to 145 km/h. It would be the third cyclone to hit Andhra Pradesh in about four weeks. Odisha has issued a red warning for 13 districts and closed schools in six until Saturday, and some trains have been rescheduled.

    The Centre told the Supreme Court that the investigation into the Ahmedabad crash is close to finished. The court gave more time and will hear the petitions for an independent inquiry in two weeks.

Something to think about

A possible new dwarf planet was hiding in old photos for ten years

The new Vera C. Rubin Observatory in Chile has found its first dwarf planet candidate, an icy world called 2026 ED138. It sits about 46 times farther from the Sun than Earth does, beyond Pluto, and may be somewhere between 600 and 1,500 km across.

The surprising part: the object had been sitting in telescope images since at least 2016. Nobody spotted it because it moves so slowly and was lost among the crowded stars of the Milky Way. Rubin's software, which compares huge numbers of images to find anything that shifts position, finally picked it out. Astronomers now need infrared observations to measure its true size.
