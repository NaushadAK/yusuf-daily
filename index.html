Top story

Democrats win control of both the US House and Senate in the midterms

The Associated Press called the House for the Democrats early on Wednesday and the Senate shortly before midnight, completing a sweep of Congress. Reported Senate gains include Texas, Maine, Ohio and North Carolina, while Mississippi and Alaska were still too close to call and could take days. President Trump signalled he would accept the results, and Vice President Vance blamed the Republicans' losses largely on the Iran war.

Something to think about

Does a daily cup of tea help your heart?

A study of nearly 486,000 adults found that people who regularly drank tea had about an 11% lower risk of heart and blood vessel disease than those who did not.

That is a large group, but the study is observational: it shows a link, not proof that tea itself is the cause. Tea drinkers may differ in diet, exercise or income in ways that also affect the heart.

Question for the day: in engineering, how would you design a fair test to separate cause from coincidence, and what would you need to keep identical between the two groups?
