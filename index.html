Top story

Attack on Saudi Arabia's Jazan refinery kills one worker

A strike on Saudi Aramco's Jazan refinery in the kingdom's south-west on Thursday started a fire, killed a Nepali worker and injured several others, Saudi Arabia's Energy Ministry said. Part of the refinery's operations were halted. The Saudi-led coalition blamed Yemen's Houthis and said it destroyed the missile launcher used, near Hodeidah. The attack came a day after the Houthis claimed a strike on the Ras Tanura refinery, and Saudi forces also said they stopped two missiles aimed at Riyadh. Oil rose above $96 a barrel, which matters to India because it imports most of its crude and millions of Indians, many from Kerala, work in the Gulf.

Something to think about

A rechargeable battery that runs on vitamin B12

Researchers at the University of Córdoba in Spain have built an experimental zinc-air battery that uses vitamin B12 as its catalyst. The cobalt atom at the centre of the vitamin helps turn oxygen from the air into water when the battery discharges, and helps reverse the reaction when it is recharged. The team fixed the vitamin onto tiny carbon nanotubes so that it would not dissolve in the battery's water-based liquid, and the cell lasted up to 250 charge cycles.

It is only a small lab cell, and it does not yet beat the best catalysts, which usually use rare and costly metals such as platinum. But cheap, safe batteries made from common materials could one day store spare solar and wind power.

Question for the day: A zinc-air battery "breathes" oxygen from the air. If you were designing its casing, how would you let air in while keeping dust, moisture and the liquid inside where they belong?
