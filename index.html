Russia and Ukraine agree to a large prisoner swap as Abu Dhabi talks end

The two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi ended on Wednesday with a deal to exchange a large number of prisoners of war. Russia's chief negotiator Vladimir Medinsky confirmed the deal, and both delegations went home to settle the exact numbers. There was no breakthrough on a ceasefire or on territory, and President Zelenskyy repeated that Ukraine will not give up occupied land in return for peace. US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff now head to Moscow to meet President Putin, and then to Dublin to meet Zelenskyy.

Something to think about

An invisible object that may be a "supermassive" neutron star

Astronomers have found a hidden object in space that gives off no light of its own but whose gravity they can detect. It may be an unusually heavy neutron star. A neutron star is the crushed core left behind when a giant star explodes: more mass than our Sun squeezed into a ball about the size of a city. A single teaspoon of it would weigh more than a mountain.

Every neutron star has a limit. Add too much mass and not even tightly packed neutrons can resist gravity, so it collapses into a black hole. Finding very heavy neutron stars tells scientists how "stiff" matter is at those extreme pressures, a bit like testing how much load a material can take before it fails, which you will study in strength of materials.

Question for the day: if you squeezed the whole Earth until it was as dense as a neutron star, roughly how big do you think it would be: a football, a building, or a city?
