Russia and Ukraine send delegations to Abu Dhabi for talks with the US

Delegations from Russia and Ukraine arrived in Abu Dhabi on Monday for US-brokered talks, set for Tuesday and Wednesday. It is the first three-way round since February, and reports say the agenda covers a possible ceasefire and security arrangements after the war. Europe's role is disputed: the European Commission president said the EU will take part, while the Kremlin insists only Russia, Ukraine and the US will be at the table.

Something to think about

Why is Diwali smog so hard to clear?

Fireworks release fine particles and gases into the air. In cool, still autumn weather the air near the ground barely moves and a layer of warmer air above acts like a lid, so the pollution stays trapped near the city instead of rising and spreading out.

Engineers use the same idea of airflow when they design chimneys, ventilation and engines.
