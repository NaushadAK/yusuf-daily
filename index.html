First day of Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi called "constructive"

Russian and Ukrainian officials talked for nearly four hours on Tuesday, with the US mediating. It was their first direct round in nine months. President Zelenskyy said the session was "constructive and quite substantive" and focused mostly on humanitarian issues such as prisoner exchanges and bringing home Ukrainian civilians held in captivity. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio called the talks "very productive" but asked for patience. The two sides are still far apart on the main terms of a deal, and the second day of talks is due today.

Something to think about

Why can a cricket ground stay unplayable after the rain stops?

Yesterday's ODI was lost to rain and a wet outfield. Modern grounds are built in layers: grass on top, then sand, then gravel and a network of perforated pipes that carry water away. Water drains through sand much faster than through clay, but if the soil underneath is already full, the water has nowhere to go. Ground staff also use machines like "super soppers", which squeeze water out of the grass with a spongy roller.

Deciding how quickly a ground drains is a real engineering problem involving porosity, pipe slope and pump capacity, the same kind of flow calculations you will meet in fluid mechanics.

Question for the day: if you had to dry a soaked cricket field in one hour, would you remove the water from above, from below, or both, and how?
